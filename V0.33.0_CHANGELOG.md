# EYE // TAIWAN v0.33.0 — Map CCTV + Dual Route

## Map-first CCTV
- After a successful place search, the nearest viewable public cameras are promoted into compact LIVE cards directly on the map.
- Cards are distance-ranked and capped to prevent camera-dense areas from covering the map.
- Existing CCTV markers, draggable enlarged popup, LIVE ANALYSIS and inline intelligence remain available.
- On phones, detailed intelligence stays collapsed after ordinary monitor searches so the map/CCTV surface remains usable.

## National flow readability
- Removed the large map hotspot labels that repeated speed values over freeway geometry.
- National hotspots are now small clickable dots while freeway segments remain the dominant green/yellow/orange/red visual.
- Exact speed, event cause and nearby CCTV remain in the national dashboard/tooltip.

## Two-route navigation
- OSRM may return up to three alternatives internally, but the UI presents at most two practical routes for easier comparison.
- Selection is distance-first; when a similarly short alternative clearly uses the other freeway corridor, it is retained to give a meaningful choice.
- Route steps are classified as 一高優先, 二高優先, mixed, or ordinary route based on actual road names/refs returned by OSRM.

## Stability
- CCTV preview loading never owns map camera movement.
- Search target movement remains the only automatic local map move.
- Service worker cache: `eye-taiwan-shell-v330`.
